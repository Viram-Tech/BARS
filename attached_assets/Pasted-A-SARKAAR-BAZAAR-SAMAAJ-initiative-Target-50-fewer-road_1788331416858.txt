Halving road deaths by 2030 — we are off track
Under SDG 3.6 and the Brasilia Declaration, India committed to a 50% reduction in road fatalities by 2030. Against the baseline, fatalities have moved just −3.8% — leaving a wide gap to close in the years remaining.

Data sources & methodology
Figures are harmonised from the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways annual "Road Accidents in India" reports, the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) and the Integrated Road Accident Database (iRAD). Annual fatality totals are calendar-year; state figures use the latest published year. Cause shares are computed on fatal-crash records and may sum beyond 100% where multiple factors apply.
